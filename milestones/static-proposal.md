# What is it like to be a woman in Mexico and how has it changed across time?

## Description

This project aims to explore how the lives of women in Mexico have changed over time and how gender gaps have evolved across different dimensions of social and economic life. Rather than focusing on a single measure of gender inequality, I would like to provide a broader picture by examining trends in areas such as education, labor market participation and earnings, unpaid and care work, political and leadership representation, and women's safety.

The project will compare outcomes for women and men whenever possible, while also incorporating indicators that are particularly relevant to women's experiences, such as femicide and other measures of gender-based violence. The goal is to identify areas in which gender gaps have narrowed, as well as dimensions in which important disparities persist or progress has been slower.
Ultimately, the project seeks to tell a visual story about the evolution of women's opportunities and well-being in Mexico, highlighting that progress toward gender equality may look very different depending on the dimension being considered.


## Data Sources

### Data Source 1: INEGI. Encuesta Nacional de Ocupación y Empleo.

URL: https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/enoe/15ymas/

Size: Varies by quarter and table; ENOE microdata contain hundreds of thousands of individual observations per quarter.

Description: Mexico's main labor market household survey, conducted by the National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI). It provides individual-level information on employment, labor force participation, occupation, working hours, labor income, and demographic characteristics. The survey is conducted quarterly and allows for comparisons by sex and over time.

### Data Source 2: INEGI. Encuesta Nacional para el Sistema de Cuidados (ENASIC) 2022.

URL: https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/enasic/2022/

Size: Varies by selected tabulation; the project will use aggregated tables rather than the full survey microdata.

Description: A household survey conducted by INEGI that provides information on the demand and provision of care in Mexico. It includes data on who provides and receives care, time spent on caregiving, support networks, and the effects of caregiving responsibilities on caregivers' lives and labor market participation, with a particular focus on women.

### Data Source 3: INEGI. Censo de Población y Vivienda. Encuesta Intercensal 2025.

URL: https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/eic/2025/#tabulados

Size: Varies by selected tabulation; the project will use aggregated tables rather than the full survey microdata.

Description: A large-scale household survey conducted by INEGI between population censuses to provide updated demographic and socioeconomic information about Mexico's population and households. It provides estimates at the national, state, and other geographic levels, with many indicators disaggregated by sex.
